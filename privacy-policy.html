We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

The App and the third-party services it uses may automatically collect the following:

Game progress, settings, and preferences (for example, your aquarium, unlocked items, sound and language settings) are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data on our servers. If you uninstall the App, this data may be deleted.

  • Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
