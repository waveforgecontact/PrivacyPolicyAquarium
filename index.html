Privacy Policy

Aquarium — Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WaveForge ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the mobile game Aquarium (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name or phone number. The only personal information we may receive directly is what you choose to send us through the optional in-app feedback form (see Section 3).

The App and the third-party services it uses may automatically collect the following:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, and screen settings.
  • Advertising identifier: such as the Google Advertising ID, used for showing and measuring ads.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: app interactions, crash reports, and performance data used to improve the game.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address (not precise GPS location).

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress, settings, and preferences (for example, your aquarium, unlocked items, and sound, music, vibration, and language settings) are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data on our servers. If you uninstall the App, this data may be deleted.

3. Feedback You Send Us

The App includes an optional feedback form in the Settings screen. Using it is entirely voluntary. When you submit the form, we receive:

  • Your message, which is the text you write.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to enter one (for example, if you would like a reply).
  • Technical context that is attached automatically to help us understand your report: the App version, your device platform and operating system version, and the game level you are on.

Feedback submissions are delivered to us through Formspree, a form-processing service, and are forwarded to our email inbox. We use this information only to read and respond to your feedback, to fix problems, and to improve the App. We do not use it for advertising.

Please do not include sensitive personal information (such as passwords or financial details) in your message. You can ask us to delete a feedback message and your email address at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 12.

4. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.
  • To fix bugs and analyze crashes and performance.
  • To display advertisements and measure their effectiveness.
  • To read and respond to feedback that you send us.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that collect or process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase (Analytics / Crashlytics)
  • Expo
  • Formspree (processes feedback form submissions)

These services may use identifiers and similar technologies to provide their features, including personalized or non-personalized advertising.

6. Advertising and Your Choices

The App may show ads. Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choices at any time by:

  • Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Opting out of ad personalization in your Google account settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information (for example, through the feedback form), please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party service providers listed above, as needed to operate the App and to process feedback you choose to send us, or when required by law.

9. Data Retention and Security

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Feedback messages and any email address you provide are kept for as long as needed to respond to you and to improve the App, and are deleted on request. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, or to withdraw consent. Since we do not maintain user accounts, most data is held by the third-party providers above or on your device. The only personal data we hold directly is feedback you have sent us. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
